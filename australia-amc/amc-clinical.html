What does this course cover?

The course covers General Medicine, Obstetrics, Gynecology, Psychiatry, Pediatrics and Physical Examination, taught live online and supported by pre-recorded lectures across multiple skills and domains.

Is the course live, or recorded?

Both. It is a live online intensive course, and you also get access to pre-recorded lectures you can work through at your own pace.

How closely does it follow the real exam?

The course is built closely on the official AMC clinical examination format, so the structure you practise in mirrors the structure you sit in.

Can I get full pathway support as well?

Yes. We also offer the Post Graduate Consulting Program, which adds mentorship, portfolio building, CV and interview preparation, English proficiency training, and full visa and logistics support.
