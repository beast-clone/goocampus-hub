What is the AMC CAT exam?

The AMC CAT exam assesses your knowledge and clinical skills to meet Australian medical standards and is required for international graduates who want to practice in Australia.

Who is this course for?

This course is designed specifically for MBBS graduates and final-year students who are preparing for the AMC CAT and seeking a Medical PG in Australia.

How is the program structured?

The program includes 90+ hours of learning, weekly live sessions, 36 hours of recorded theory, six mock tests, recall sessions, and a trial exam.

Can I get full pathway support as well?

Yes, in addition to the standalone program, we also offer a complete pathway service, including visa support, job guidance, and more.

Do I need prior experience or knowledge of the Australian healthcare system?

No prior experience in the Australian healthcare system is required. This program is designed to guide you from the basics to advanced topics, making it suitable for any international medical graduate.

Is there a specific schedule, and can I take the course at my own pace?

We have a structured 3-month schedule with weekly live classes. You can also access recorded sessions, allowing you to learn at your own pace alongside our recommended schedule.
