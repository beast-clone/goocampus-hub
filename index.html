About GooCampus

Guiding doctors end to end, wherever they are in the process

GooCampus is a medical career consulting and training firm helping doctors plan their path abroad, across India, Australia, USA, the Gulf and beyond. From eligibility checks and choosing the right pathway to licensing, exams, documentation and career readiness, we guide doctors end to end, with the same care and attention whether they are just starting out or already deep into the process.
