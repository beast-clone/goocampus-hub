FAQ /

Questions, answered

Still unsure? Call 080-41743956 and a counsellor will talk it through.

I don't know which country suits me. Where do I start?

With an eligibility check. We look at your degree, scores and goals, then tell you honestly which pathway fits, before you sign up for anything.

Do you help with exams, or only with paperwork?

Both. Each program covers exam prep guidance (NEET PG, AMC, USMLE, Prometric, German and FSP), plus documents, registration and the job search.

Can I speak to doctors already working abroad?

Yes. Mentoring sessions with practising doctors abroad are part of the programs, so you hear how the pathway really works.

How much does it cost, and how long does it take?

It depends on the country and where you are in the process. Your counsellor gives you clear timelines and costs in your pathway plan, up front.

I'm still an MBBS student. Is it too early?

No. Starting early helps you time exams and documents well. We work with students and graduates at every stage.
