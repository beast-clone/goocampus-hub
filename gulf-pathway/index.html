Who We Are

Physician-led, end to end

GooCampus is a medical career consulting and training firm helping doctors plan their PG path abroad — across the Gulf, Australia, UK, and USA. We guide you through licensing, exam prep, documentation, and career readiness, end to end.

Every program is led by physicians who have already made this move. You work one-on-one with mentors practicing in the Gulf right now — through licensing, documentation, and the job search that follows.
